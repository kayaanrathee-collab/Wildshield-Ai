THE TECHNOLOGY

Intelligence that watches
where humans can't.

WildShield AI combines camera-based computer vision, intelligent animal detection and road-side warning systems to create an early-alert layer between wildlife and vehicles.

👁️

AI Vision

A camera observes the roadside environment and an AI model analyses the scene for animals.

🦌

Animal Detection

The system identifies wildlife entering areas where vehicles may be approaching.

⚠️

Early Warning

When an animal is detected, the system can trigger a warning mechanism for approaching drivers.
